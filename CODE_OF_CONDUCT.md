# Community Code of Conduct

We want NO ESCAPE to be a welcoming, practical place to learn and contribute. Treat everyone with respect, especially beginners and people with different bodies, abilities, experience levels, and access to equipment.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a healthy community include:

- using welcoming, inclusive language;
- offering constructive feedback and accepting it gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the community and its users;
- respecting privacy and not sharing somebody else's account details, health information, or video;
- discussing exercise guidance without shaming, diagnosing, or making assumptions about a person's body.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, and demeaning comments about bodies or abilities.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces, issues, pull requests, and community interactions representing the project. Repository maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when this is necessary to keep a safe and constructive community. Contact the repository maintainers privately using the reporting method provided by the hosting organization.
